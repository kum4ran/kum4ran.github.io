The Short Version

01 — BIO

Son Heung-min grew up in Chuncheon under the relentless, almost monastic coaching of his father, a former pro footballer who drilled him for hours a day on ball control before he ever played a competitive match. That foundation took him to Germany as a teenager, first at Hamburger SV and then Bayer Leverkusen, before Tottenham Hotspur signed him in 2015 for what was then a club-record fee for an Asian player.

Over the next decade he became the most decorated Asian player in Premier League history — sharing the Golden Boot in 2021/22, becoming the first Asian to reach 100 league goals, and being named Spurs captain in 2023. In August 2025 he left north London for Los Angeles FC, a move he has described as partly a deliberate step to prepare for playing on North American soil ahead of the 2026 World Cup. He captains South Korea into that tournament, his fourth as a Taegeuk Warrior.

Off the pitch, Son is known as one of football's most consistently humble superstars — soft-spoken, close with his family, and just as likely to be seen fetching cones at training as scoring wonder goals in front of 60,000 people.

Scouting Report

02 — FACTS
01

Shared the 2021/22 Premier League Golden Boot with Mohamed Salah, both finishing on 23 goals — the first time an Asian player had topped the league's scoring charts.

02

Won the 2020 Puskás Award for a solo, coast-to-coast run and finish against Burnley — voted the best goal in world football that year.

03

Became the first Asian player to score 100 Premier League goals, capping a rise that also made him the club's fifth-highest scorer of all time.

04

Played the entire 2022 World Cup in a custom carbon-fibre mask after fracturing his eye socket weeks earlier, captaining South Korea into the knockout rounds regardless.

05

Left Tottenham in August 2025 after a decade to join LAFC — a move he's said was partly timed to get used to North American conditions before the 2026 World Cup.

06

Scored twice inside the first 23 minutes of the 2026 MLS All-Star Game to win MVP honours in his first season in Major League Soccer.
